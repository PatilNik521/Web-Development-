10 Mathematical & Scientific Formulas

  1. Area of square = a2
  2. Area of circle = πr2
  3. Pythagoras theorem = a2 + b2 = c2
  4. Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100
  5. Compound Interest = P(1 + R/100)n
  6. Speed = Distance / Time
  7. Quadratic equation = ax2 + bx + c = 0
  8. Water formula = H2O
  9. Carbon dioxide = CO2
  10. Einstein’s equation = E = mc2
